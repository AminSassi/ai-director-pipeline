## Customization Guide

Replace or adjust elements based on episode tone:

| Element | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **BPM** | 60-70 (slow dread), 80-90 (investigative), 100-110 (heist/action) |
| **Opening** | low digital tone, single piano note, distant siren, static crackle |
| **Build** | synthetic pulse, rising strings, ticking clock, heartbeat bass |
| **Peak** | explosive metallic percussion, rapid ostinato, orchestral crash |
| **Pullback** | sparse cello, distant hum, suspended note, near-silence |
| **Texture** | film-grain, analog warmth, glitchy interference, tape hiss |
| **Tags** | dark ambient, industrial, orchestral, electronic, experimental |

---

## Episode Variations

### Corporate Collapse (NMC, Wirecard, Steinhoff)
Use base template as-is. Cold precision, financial thriller tone.

### Heist / Cybercrime (Bangladesh Bank, Ransomware)
Increase BPM to 80-90. Add: fast sequencer arpeggios, digital data streams, alarm synth stabs.

### Human Tragedy
Slow to 55-60 BPM. Add: solo violin, mournful cello, ambient field recordings, emotional swells.

### Political / Government
Add: military snare, brass drones, patriotic undertone distorted, news broadcast static.

---

## Rules
- Always end with `[Instrumental]`
- Keep tags at end for Suno genre detection
- Never exceed 200 words in prompt
- Match energy arc to episode structure: Hook (high) → Setup (medium) → Build (tension) → Peak (high) → Resolution (low)
