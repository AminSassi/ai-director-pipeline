## Asset Usage Rules

1. **Always tag locked assets at BEGINNING of prompt** — even if character only appears in final shot
2. **Tag format:** @TAGNAME at start, then description
3. **Never put tags in middle of prompt**
4. **Example:** "@JHOLOW Young Malaysian man walking through nightclub..." NOT "Young Malaysian man @JHOLOW walking through..."
