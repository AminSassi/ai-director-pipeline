### Description

For 4,500 years, the sands of the Giza Plateau have kept their secrets. Millions of tourists and thousands of archaeologists have walked across this ground, believing every stone was mapped and understood.

They were looking in the wrong place.

In 2025, a joint team of Japanese and Egyptian researchers led by Professor Motoyuki Sato of Tohoku University deployed advanced Ground-Penetrating Radar (GPR) and Electrical Resistivity Tomography (ERT) across an overlooked, empty stretch of the Western Cemetery.

Just two meters beneath the surface, they detected something that shouldn't exist: a razor-sharp, 10-meter by 10-meter L-shaped structure with perfect man-made right angles. Even deeper—between 5 and 10 meters down—the equipment hit a massive, highly resistive anomaly: an unexplored subterranean chamber or void.

Is it a lost royal tomb? A ceremonial entrance to a vast underground complex? Or the first physical evidence of something far older?

This is the full scientific investigation into the Giza L-Shaped Anomaly—separating viral sensationalism from verified archaeological truth.
